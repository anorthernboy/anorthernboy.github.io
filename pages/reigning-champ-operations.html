Behind the storefront, eCommerce at Reigning Champ is one person, an external dev team and a queue of requests from every department. This is what the job looks like when a warehouse moves, customs rules keep changing and the World Cup arrives.

How work runsFrom request to release

With every team relying on one person, the process matters as much as the design. I set up a delivery rhythm that the business and our external developers both work to.

  1. Intake

    Requests from publishing, merchandising and product teams.

  2. Prioritise

    Shaped into a roadmap, epics and a single backlog.

  3. Design

    Built from the design system, to brand guidelines.

  4. Build

    Delivered in sprints with the external dev team.

  5. Test

    End-to-end UAT on every feature, by me, before release.

  6. Measure

    Performance tracked in GA4, feeding the next round.

When things changeOperations

  1. Logistics

    Moving the DC across Canada

    Customer experience through a warehouse move

    Worked with senior leaders on timelines and risk, and kept customers informed across product pages, checkout and email.

  2. Trade

    Responding to US tariffs

    Split shipping from a US DC

    With logistics and distribution, navigated changing customs rules, configured split shipping from a US warehouse and reconfigured the returns app for the new process.

  3. Checkout

    Discounts & gift with purchase

    Custom Shopify apps

    An end-to-end gift-with-purchase experience on a customised app, plus employee discount schemes through automatic discounts and custom checkout apps.

Brand momentsLaunches & activations

Alongside the day to day, I designed and launched the pages that carry the brand's biggest moments, each to brand guidelines and on the same design system.

  1. FIFA World Cup hub

    An experiential hub with brand information, activations, stores and city guides.

  2. PUMA collaboration

    A branded activation page for the collaboration launch.

  3. Lookbooks and gift guides

    Seasonal editorial pages that blend storytelling with shopping.

  4. Wishlist

    A sitewide wishlist app, custom-designed to fit the brand.

OutcomeGrowth without a bigger team

Over two years the business grew from $20m to $30m CAD, and it's targeting $40m next year. eCommerce kept pace with one in-house person, because the process, the platform and the design system carry the load.

Lesson

Operational work is design work. A tariff change or a warehouse move is a customer experience problem first, and it needs the same care as a new page.
