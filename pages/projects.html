Ten projects,no further reading

  1. lululemon – Rethinking “you may also like” as head-to-toe outfitting.

    Product pages recommend similar items, a pattern ripe for disruption. I sketched how lululemon could suggest complete looks instead, through outfit building, a smarter wishlist and filterable recommendations.

    eCommerce · Ideation · Sketching
    1/4

  2. Arc'teryx – Taking a great comparison feature further.

    Arc'teryx lets shoppers compare up to three products without leaving the page. I explored where it could go next: quick filters, recommended picks, a “best for” view and a generative AI product expert.

    eCommerce · Ideation · Generative AI
    1/4

  3. Pantrii – A smart vending machine for zero-waste living.

    Food and plastic are the biggest categories of household waste. Pantrii is a proof of concept for smart vending machines in city apartment blocks, paired with an app to track purchases and progress.

    Service design · App · Sketching
    1/4

  4. Spotify – What if you could tune your own recommendations?

    Using the Spotify API and k-means clustering, I tested a hunch that recommendations lean too heavily on popularity, then proposed a control that lets listeners adjust the engine.

    Data · Clustering · Design thinking
    1/4

  5. GoodBox Reporting – Finding the number the business was missing.

    Live dashboards in SQL and Power BI showed a scaling fundraising startup was falling 10x short on its core KPI. That triggered a strategic pivot and a 30–40x rise in recurring revenue.

    Product management · SQL · Power BI
    1/4

  6. seemless – Crowdsourced fabric to cut fashion overproduction.

    seemless lets independent designers pool demand to reach minimum fabric runs, reducing speculative production. I designed the brand, the landing page and a dashboard.

    Branding · Concept · UI
    1/3

  7. GoodGiving – One place for a charity’s digital fundraising.

    As product manager and designer, I took a payment platform from client interviews to beta, consolidating charities’ fundraising tools for financial reporting.

    Product management · Research · MVP
    1/3

  8. City Giving Day – Participatory fundraising for 400+ corporations.

    For the Lord Mayor’s Appeal, I designed and built an MVP where colleagues nominated each other for challenges, with a real-time calculator showing each donation’s impact.

    Frontend · Prototyping · UI
    1/3

  9. Prevayl – Sizing up the market for smart clothing.

    Prevayl turns body data into health and fitness guidance. I assessed how the product could shift its market and competitors, then recommended how to develop it.

    Strategy · Vision · Design thinking
    1/3

  10. Hoptions – Craft beer picked for your taste.

    An app that builds a taste profile from the beers you like, then uses image recognition and recommendations to find your next one.

    App · Concept · UI
    1/3
