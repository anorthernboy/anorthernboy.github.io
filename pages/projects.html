Concept
1/5

Spotify – What if you could tune your own recommendations?

Self-initiated
1/6

Using the Spotify API and k-means clustering, I tested a hunch that recommendations lean too heavily on popularity. My own playlists turned out to be consistent in sound rather than artist, so I proposed a control that lets listeners adjust the engine.

Data · Clustering · Design thinking

1/6

Product management · SQL · Power BI
