Craig WeighmanPhotographed 2026

Design-led product generalist. Sixteen years making products, the last seven designing digital ones. I take eCommerce work from the first question to the final QA pass, with an eye for detail and a soft spot for Dieter Rams. Also a shoegaze guitarist.

PracticeHow I work

I work across digital experience design and product management, usually as the person who connects the two. Research, problem definition, interface design and delivery sit with me from start to finish.

A design system in Figma and development-ready files keep engineers moving in an agile process.

Decisions come from evidence. I run user research, read product performance in GA4, and use both to set priorities.

Before anything ships I QA it end to end, because the last ten percent is where a product feels finished.
