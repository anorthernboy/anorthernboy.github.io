Reigning Champ makes some of the best-built athletic wear there is, and its website didn't feel that way. As the only person in eCommerce, I moved the store to Shopify 2.0, then redesigned the pages where people actually shop, without losing a point of conversion along the way.

  • 2.0%Conversion target held through migration and both redesigns
  • $30mRevenue, up from $20m CAD over two years
  • 60+eCommerce sites reviewed before redesigning
  • 1In-house eCommerce team member, with an external dev team

Starting pointA team of one

I joined as the first in-house UX/UI designer and the whole eCommerce team, reporting to the Digital Director. Development sat with an external agency, and requests arrived from publishing, merchandising and product.

The store ran on a legacy theme, and its product data needed remodelling before anything else could move quickly. The platform had to come first.

I owned
  • Roadmap, epics and backlog
  • Research and GA4 analysis
  • Design and design system
  • Delivery with external developers
  • End-to-end UAT

The platformShopify 2.0 migration

I led the migration end to end: design, delivery and remodelling the legacy data. Three things made everything after it faster.

  1. Design system

    An atomic system in Figma, so new pages were assembled from parts, not drawn from scratch.

  2. Content model

    Metaobjects and product metafields let the publishing team reuse product stories across the catalogue.

  3. Delivery rhythm

    A sprint process that lined up internal teams and external partners on one backlog.

Research60+ sites, four principles

Before touching the collection and product pages, I reviewed more than 60 eCommerce sites. Four principles came out of it and guided every decision after.

  • Less to think about

    Reduce cognitive load on every page.

  • Room to breathe

    Whitespace is part of what makes a brand feel premium.

  • Story and commerce

    Blend brand storytelling into the shopping, not beside it.

  • Browsing first

    Design for people exploring, not only people ready to buy.

Collection pageWhere browsing happens

Product pageStory higher up

Reigning Champ's difference is in fabric, construction and fit, and that story sat too far down the page. A horizontal gallery freed up the page, moving storytelling and product knowledge up to where people make decisions.

OutcomeHolding the line

Conversion held at its 2.0% target through the migration and both page redesigns. That happened alongside double-digit revenue growth, global political turmoil and a large influx of new customers from increased acquisition marketing.

New visitors usually convert lower than loyal ones, so holding the rate while the audience grew meant the new pages were doing more work. Over two years, revenue grew from $20m to $30m CAD, with $40m targeted next year.

Lesson

Fix the platform before the pages. The migration, design system and content model were invisible to customers, but they're why the redesigns shipped quickly and safely.
